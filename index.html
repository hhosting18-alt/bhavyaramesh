    [The maker]

    BHAVYA RAMESH

    Established in 2018, Bhavya Ramesh is dedicated to the artistry of traditional jewellery making. Led by founder Bhavya Ramesh, we prioritise the human touch, supporting local artisans who craft each piece with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that our jewellery is not only desirable but also meant to be cherished and passed down through generations.

    Rooted in the values of Indian culture, we foster a sense of community and craftsmanship while continuously pushing the boundaries of fashion. Operating from our workshop in Jaipur, we proudly employ a team of 40 skilled artisans and 10 dedicated workers, supported by a team of 30 in Mumbai. Renowned for excellence and experimentation in silver jewellery, our designs resonate with discerning customers worldwide.
